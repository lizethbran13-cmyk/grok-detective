HOW TO PLAY

  1. Explore 5 places: Town Square, Mansion, Museum, Docks and Forest Cabin. Use the MAP to travel.
  2. Search anything with a ? above it. Walk up and tap SEARCH, or just tap the object.
  3. Talk to people with a … bubble. Suspects tell you about themselves. Witnesses give clues.
  4. Your CASEBOOK keeps every clue and what you learned about each suspect.
  5. Stuck? Tap LENS: your magnifying glass makes hidden clues glow.
  6. When you know who did it, tap ACCUSE: pick the culprit and the evidence that proves it. Wrong guesses give you a hint.
  7. Co-op: every clue a partner finds goes into everyone's casebook. Accusations need everyone to agree.

The culprit and clue spots change every time you play!
